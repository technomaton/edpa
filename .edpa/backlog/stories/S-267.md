`renumber_collisions.py` takes the replacement ID from the allocator
(ledger reservation under the remote authority), stops renumbering a
branch's own squash-merged item, and lets `depends_on` and iteration
lists follow a rename. `--check` reports unreserved items for CI.

The PI planning server's `nextId` — a second, uncoordinated allocator —
calls `id_counter.py next` instead.
