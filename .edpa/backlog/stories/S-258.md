Follow-up to D-82 / S-256 known limitation: when the gate-event
attribution chain falls through to step (2) `passthrough` (parent's
all-time contributors[], engine.py ~:1170), per-iteration shares are
coarse — the passthrough ablation reproduces ~47 % worst case. Today
this happens silently.

Scope: at iteration close, count gate events by attribution label
(window / passthrough / transition-author — labels already exist at
engine.py ~:1216) and record the counts + ratio into the audit section
of `edpa_results.json`, following the `load_story_activity_events`
pattern (engine.py:824 — returns `(events, audit)`, audit logged into
results). Emit a stderr WARN when the passthrough ratio exceeds a
configurable threshold.

This is diagnostics, not a fix of passthrough granularity — it makes
the residual observable so a future fix can be prioritized on data.
Mirror copy `plugin/edpa/scripts/engine.py` must stay in sync.
