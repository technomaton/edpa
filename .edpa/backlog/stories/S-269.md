Optional extension — awaiting a decision, not implemented.

Today, and after the rest of this feature, a Story can only be created
under a parent whose file exists in the checkout: a Feature created in
one worktree cannot get Stories from another worktree until its branch
is merged. The reservation record already carries type, title and
parent, so `edpa_item_create` could accept a parent that is reserved but
still lives on another branch, and `backlog tree` could list such
reservations ("reserved elsewhere").

Measured need on the consumer pilot: of 155 Stories and Defects created
in 30 days, 120 had a parent older than a week (long on main) and 12 one
younger than an hour (same session). About 23 (15 %) fall in between and
may have had their parent on another, unmerged branch.

Referencing only — the parent's content and status would still not be
visible or editable from the other worktree.
